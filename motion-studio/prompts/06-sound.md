# Score it to the beat (Step 8)

If you have a track, measure it: `python beats.py song.wav > beats.json`. Screen changes land on
beats, big moments on downbeats, and sound effects on the hits.

If you don't have a track, let Opus compose one:

```
Synthesize original music at 120 BPM in code, plus click, pop, thump, and
whoosh sound effects. Write a cue list of every sound effect timed to the beat
grid, mix it all to -14 LUFS, and combine it with out/silent.mp4 into
out/final.mp4.
```

Only use music you have the rights to. Code-composed music or royalty-free tracks keep the videos
safe to post.
